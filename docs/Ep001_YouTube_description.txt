How Steve Jobs Helped Save Apple | EMPIRE STORIES Ep. 001

In 1997, Michael Dell said he'd shut Apple down. That year Apple lost $1 billion. Ten years later it earned $3.5 billion. What actually changed, and how much of it was one man?
