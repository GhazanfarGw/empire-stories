# Five-Channel Media Company Plan
Owner decision: 21 Nov 2026 · Prepared by the channel's AI production agent (CEO role)

## Guiding rules
- **One new channel every 6–8 weeks**, launched only when the previous one runs smoothly. Never all at once.
- **Quality protects monetization.** YouTube does not monetize mass-produced, template-looking content. Each channel has its own name, look, voice, research and manual. The production system is shared, but the channels must not look like copies of each other.
- **YPP deadline:** from 1 Feb 2027 new channels need 1,000 subscribers + 8,000 watch hours (12 months) or 20M Shorts views (90 days), up from 4,000 hours / 10M. Until then, 100% of effort goes into Empire Stories.
- **Capacity limits today:** one render pipeline (~40 min render per 15-min documentary, 2 CPUs) and 5 uploads per day across all channels (Zapier free plan). Upgrade the upload path before channel 3.
- **Shared back office for all channels:** research → script → fact-check → voice → render → Shorts → upload → localization (auto-dubs + translated titles) → backups (GitHub, Drive, E:, Project).

## Channel 1 — EMPIRE STORIES (live)
- Company documentaries, 10–25 min, 3 per week (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 1 Short daily.
- Slate: `SLATE_TOP_100.md`. Approved house style: Operations Manual.
- Until 1 Feb 2027: everything on watch hours (playlists, end screens, every Short linking to its episode, A/B thumbnails).

## Channel 2 — Economics channel (target launch: Feb 2027)
- **Concept:** why countries get rich or poor; inflation, debt, oil, currencies, trade. E.g. "Why Pakistan's rupee keeps falling", "How Dubai got rich", "Why Japan's economy froze".
- **Why second:** highest-paying niche; reuses our chart/map/timeline engine with a new brand.
- **Name ideas:** Wealth of Nations · The Money Map · Economics Unfolded.
- **Prep in January 2027:** brand, logo, colours, voice, first 10 titles, 2 finished episodes ready for launch day.

## Channel 3 — Biographies (target launch: ~Apr 2027)
- **Concept:** life journeys of CEOs, business leaders, cricketers, footballers, Bollywood/Hollywood actors: birth, schooling, struggle, turning point, status in the present year.
- **Why third:** huge search demand and a nearly endless subject list. Already reserved in the project rules as the separate channel for individual biographies (kept out of Empire Stories).
- **Rules:** licensed photos only (Wikimedia/press kits); AI illustrations always labelled; facts only from reputable sources; no rumours or private-life speculation about living people.
- **Name ideas:** Legends Unfolded · The Rise Of · Made Famous.

## Channel 4 — Geopolitics & war documentaries (target launch: ~Jun 2027)
- **Concept:** why wars started, borders, oil, alliances, the Cold War, China–US, South Asian history, told neutrally.
- **Rules:** documentary context is required for monetization; use maps, archive photos and animation, no gore; present every side's view fairly with sources; no sensationalism.
- **Name ideas:** World Order · The Map Room · Fault Lines.

## Channel 5 — Religions & spiritual figures (target launch: later 2027)
- **Concept:** the great religions of the world, their history and holy places, and famous saints and spiritual figures across every faith, told respectfully.
- **Strict rules (highest sensitivity):**
  - every script reviewed by a qualified scholar before publishing
  - no visual depiction of prophets (calligraphy, architecture, maps and manuscripts instead)
  - no ranking of religions against each other; no sectarian debates
  - every claim sourced
- **Name ideas:** Sacred Journeys · Faiths of the World · Noor Stories.

## Per-channel setup checklist
1. YouTube Brand Account under the owner's Google account (2-step verification, backup codes, a second trusted manager).
2. Name, logo, banner, watermark, colours, fonts, narrator voice (distinct from the other channels).
3. Channel manual derived from the Empire Stories manual (sound rules, cinematic style, sources, fact-check).
4. First 10 titles + 2 finished episodes before launch.
5. Upload automation, localization, backups.
